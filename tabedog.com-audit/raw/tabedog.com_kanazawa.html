Best restaurants in Kanazawa

Kanazawa is the best seafood city in Japan that most visitors have never eaten in. The Sea of Japan coast delivers nodoguro (blackthroat seaperch), snow crab in winter, sweet shrimp and yellowtail to Omicho Market every morning, and the city's sushi counters serve fish of Tokyo quality at half the price with no waiting list. Kaga cuisine, the local kaiseki tradition built on the Maeda clan's wealth, is served in old samurai and geisha districts.

Omicho Market is the center of everything and the kaisendon stalls inside it are rated with precision on Tabelog, which matters because the ones at the busiest entrances are not the ones locals queue at.

Where to eat in Kanazawa, by neighborhood

  • Omicho Market the 300-year-old seafood market with kaisendon stalls and sushi counters
  • Higashi Chaya the geisha district: tea houses, gold-leaf sweets and kaiseki
  • Katamachi & Korinbo the downtown with izakaya, oden bars and sushi counters
  • Kanazawa Station the modern hub with a strong food hall
  • Nagamachi the samurai district with quiet Kaga cuisine restaurants

What to eat in Kanazawa

  • Kaisendon sweet shrimp, crab, nodoguro and yellowtail over rice at Omicho
  • Nodoguro the fatty blackthroat seaperch, grilled or as nigiri
  • Kaga kaiseki the local haute cuisine with jibuni duck stew and gold leaf
  • Kanazawa oden winter oden with crab shells and local vegetables
  • Hanton rice omurice topped with fried fish and tartar sauce, a Kanazawa yoshoku original

Kanazawa restaurant FAQ

What food is Kanazawa famous for?

Kaisendon, Nodoguro, Kaga kaiseki, Kanazawa oden, Hanton rice. Sweet shrimp, crab, nodoguro and yellowtail over rice at Omicho.

Where should I eat in Kanazawa?

Omicho Market for the 300-year-old seafood market with kaisendon stalls and sushi counters; Higashi Chaya for the geisha district: tea houses, gold-leaf sweets and kaiseki; Katamachi & Korinbo for the downtown with izakaya, oden bars and sushi counters. The pages for sushi, seafood, kaiseki in Kanazawa go neighborhood by neighborhood.

What is a good Tabelog rating in Kanazawa?

Kanazawa sushi counters at 3.6+ would be 3.9 in Tokyo; book them. Omicho kaisendon stalls at 3.4+ are excellent. Kaga kaiseki houses sit around 3.6 to 3.9 and lunch is the way in.

Is Tabelog available in English for Kanazawa?

Tabelog has a limited English site, but most listings, reviews and the useful details (hours, prices, payment methods) are only in Japanese. TabeDog shows Kanazawa's restaurants on an English map with those details translated, using Tabelog's own ratings.

How do I avoid tourist traps in Kanazawa?

Use the Tabelog score, not the sign. Restaurants with big English signs and touts near Omicho Market typically rate 3.0 or lower; the ones locals queue for rate 3.4 and up and are often a street or two away. TabeDog puts both on the same map so the difference is visible.
