Best sushi in Tokyo

Tokyo is the sushi capital of the world and Ginza alone has more 3.8+ sushi counters than most countries. The move for visitors is lunch omakase in Ginza or Nihonbashi for ¥5,000 to ¥10,000 at counters that charge three times that at night. For a cheaper fix, the Tsukiji outer market and the standing sushi bars around Shinbashi serve excellent nigiri from early morning.
