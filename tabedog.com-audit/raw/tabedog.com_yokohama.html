Best restaurants in Yokohama

Yokohama is thirty minutes from Tokyo and has two things the capital does not: Japan's largest Chinatown, with over 200 restaurants, and iekei ramen, the thick pork-and-soy style with spinach and seaweed that was invented at a single shop near Shin-Yokohama and now has thousands of imitators. The Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum collects regional ramen under one roof, and Noge, a drinking district by Sakuragicho, is one of the best izakaya streets in the Tokyo area.

Yokohama Chinatown is where Tabelog earns its keep: 200 restaurants, most mediocre, a dozen genuinely excellent, and the difference invisible from the street.

Where to eat in Yokohama, by neighborhood

  • Chinatown (Chukagai) the largest in Japan; steamed buns, dim sum, Cantonese banquets
  • Minato Mirai the waterfront with harbor-view restaurants and the Red Brick Warehouse
  • Yokohama Station department-store food floors and the city's commuter dining
  • Noge a dense, old-school drinking and izakaya district near Sakuragicho
  • Shin-Yokohama the Ramen Museum and the birthplace of iekei ramen

Yokohama restaurant FAQ

What food is Yokohama famous for?

Iekei ramen, Sanmamen, Dim sum and buns, Napolitan spaghetti. Thick tonkotsu-shoyu with spinach, nori and a choice of richness.

Where should I eat in Yokohama?

Chinatown (Chukagai) for the largest in Japan; steamed buns, dim sum, Cantonese banquets; Minato Mirai for the waterfront with harbor-view restaurants and the Red Brick Warehouse; Yokohama Station for department-store food floors and the city's commuter dining. What is a good Tabelog rating in Yokohama?

Chinatown restaurants are graded harshly; 3.5 there is a top-tier kitchen and 3.3 is a safe pick. Iekei ramen shops rate 3.3 to 3.6 and the originator family shops are easy to spot by score. Noge izakaya at 3.4+ are excellent.

Is Tabelog available in English for Yokohama?

Tabelog has a limited English site, but most listings, reviews and the useful details (hours, prices, payment methods) are only in Japanese. TabeDog shows Yokohama's restaurants on an English map with those details translated, using Tabelog's own ratings.

How do I avoid tourist traps in Yokohama?

Use the Tabelog score, not the sign. Restaurants with big English signs and touts near Chinatown (Chukagai) typically rate 3.0 or lower; the ones locals queue for rate 3.4 and up and are often a street or two away. TabeDog puts both on the same map so the difference is visible.
