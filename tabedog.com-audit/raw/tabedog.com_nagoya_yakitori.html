Best yakitori in Nagoya

Nagoya's chicken dish is tebasaki, wings fried and glazed in a sweet, peppery sauce, eaten by the plate in Sakae izakaya with beer. The two rival originators are both in Sakae. For proper charcoal yakitori using the local Nagoya Cochin breed, the counters around Nagoya Station and Kanayama are the top-rated.

Where to find the best yakitori in Nagoya

The neighborhoods where Tabelog's highest-rated yakitori in Nagoya clusters:

  • Sakae the downtown: tebasaki, izakaya, bars and the city's nightlife
  • Nagoya Station department-store floors, the station's kishimen stands, and the easiest place to try everything
  • Kanayama a transit hub with a dense, local izakaya scene
