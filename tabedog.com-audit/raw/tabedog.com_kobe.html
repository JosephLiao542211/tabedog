Best restaurants in Kobe

Kobe is a port city with the most famous beef in the world, a Chinatown (Nankinmachi), a historic foreign quarter (Kitano) full of French and Indian restaurants, and the Nada sake district, which brews a quarter of Japan's sake. Kobe beef is the reason most visitors come, and it is where Tabelog is essential: only restaurants registered with the Kobe Beef Marketing Association serve the certified product, and the ratings separate them from the 'Kobe-style' steak shops.

Sannomiya is the center and everything is walkable from it: Nankinmachi to the west, Kitano up the hill, the harbor to the south. Lunch courses at the top teppanyaki counters cost about half of dinner for the same beef.

Kobe restaurant FAQ

What food is Kobe famous for?

Kobe beef, Sobameshi, Butaman, Nada sake. Certified Tajima-breed wagyu, seared on a teppan.

Where should I eat in Kobe?

Sannomiya for the downtown hub: teppanyaki, izakaya, bars and the main station; Motomachi & Nankinmachi for Kobe's Chinatown and the shopping arcade beside it; Kitano for the hillside foreign quarter with Western-style houses, bakeries and bistros. The pages for wagyu & teppanyaki, yakiniku, chinese in Kobe go neighborhood by neighborhood.

What is a good Tabelog rating in Kobe?

Kobe's steak ranking is the list to use: 3.6+ teppanyaki counters are serving certified beef with the ID certificate on display. For everything else Kobe graders are moderate; 3.4 is a good sign.

Is Tabelog available in English for Kobe?

Tabelog has a limited English site, but most listings, reviews and the useful details (hours, prices, payment methods) are only in Japanese. TabeDog shows Kobe's restaurants on an English map with those details translated, using Tabelog's own ratings.

How do I avoid tourist traps in Kobe?

Use the Tabelog score, not the sign. Restaurants with big English signs and touts near Sannomiya typically rate 3.0 or lower; the ones locals queue for rate 3.4 and up and are often a street or two away. TabeDog puts both on the same map so the difference is visible.
