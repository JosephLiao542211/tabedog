Best japanese curry in Osaka

Osaka invented spice curry, the aromatic multi-curry plate that has taken over Japan, and the scene is centered on Shinsaibashi, Horie and Tanimachi. Most shops are lunch-only, cash-only, and sell out; the top-rated ones have lines of office workers by 11:45.
