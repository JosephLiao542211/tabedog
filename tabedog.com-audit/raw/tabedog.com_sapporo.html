Best restaurants in Sapporo

Sapporo eats like a northern port city: miso ramen was invented here, soup curry was invented here, and the seafood that comes off Hokkaido boats (crab, sea urchin, salmon roe, scallops) ends up in kaisendon bowls at Nijo Market and the Curb Market before 9 a.m. Jingisukan, lamb grilled on a domed iron plate, is the beer-hall meal. Susukino, the entertainment district, has ramen alleys open until dawn.

Sapporo's dining is compact: nearly everything worth eating is between Sapporo Station and Susukino, a twenty-minute walk. Tabelog ratings matter most for the seafood market stalls, where the tourist-facing ones near the entrance are the lowest rated, and for ramen, where the famous Ramen Yokocho alley is outscored by shops a few blocks away.

Where to eat in Sapporo, by neighborhood

  • Susukino the entertainment district: Ramen Yokocho, jingisukan halls, late-night everything
  • Sapporo Station & Odori department-store food floors, soup curry, and the central business lunch scene
  • Nijo Market central seafood market for kaisendon breakfasts
  • Curb Market (Jogai) the larger wholesale market near Nijuyonken with crab and uni stalls
  • Maruyama a leafy residential area with the city's best cafes and bakeries

What to eat in Sapporo

  • Miso ramen invented in Sapporo in the 1950s; thick, buttery, with corn and sweetcorn
  • Soup curry a thin spiced broth with roasted vegetables and chicken on the bone
  • Jingisukan lamb grilled on a domed plate, named after Genghis Khan
  • Kaisendon uni, ikura and crab over rice from the morning markets
  • Zangi Hokkaido's heavily seasoned fried chicken

Sapporo restaurant FAQ

What food is Sapporo famous for?

Miso ramen, Soup curry, Jingisukan, Kaisendon, Zangi. Invented in Sapporo in the 1950s; thick, buttery, with corn and sweetcorn.

Where should I eat in Sapporo?

Susukino for the entertainment district: Ramen Yokocho, jingisukan halls, late-night everything; Sapporo Station & Odori for department-store food floors, soup curry, and the central business lunch scene; Nijo Market for central seafood market for kaisendon breakfasts. The pages for ramen, seafood, japanese curry in Sapporo go neighborhood by neighborhood.

What is a good Tabelog rating in Sapporo?

Hokkaido reviewers rate seafood with expert eyes: a 3.4 kaisendon shop is a strong pick. Miso ramen at 3.5+ is a destination bowl. Jingisukan halls are rated for atmosphere as much as lamb, so read the price band too.

Is Tabelog available in English for Sapporo?

Tabelog has a limited English site, but most listings, reviews and the useful details (hours, prices, payment methods) are only in Japanese. TabeDog shows Sapporo's restaurants on an English map with those details translated, using Tabelog's own ratings.

How do I avoid tourist traps in Sapporo?

Use the Tabelog score, not the sign. Restaurants with big English signs and touts near Susukino typically rate 3.0 or lower; the ones locals queue for rate 3.4 and up and are often a street or two away. TabeDog puts both on the same map so the difference is visible.
